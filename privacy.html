Privacy Policy

Era Siege — Ad-free App Store edition Last updated: October 28, 2026 This is an offline single-player game. Playing requires no account or internet connection. We do not collect or transmit personal information, device identifiers or gameplay data to our servers. This edition has no advertising, analytics or tracking and does not include Google's advertising libraries. Progress, settings and resumable battles are saved locally on your device. We do not upload these files to a server. Removing the app's data removes these local saves. Apple may manage device backups, store information and diagnostics under your device settings and Apple's policies. The game does not request camera, microphone, photo, location or tracking permission. There are no purchases or subscriptions. A future release that changes these practices will update this policy and the store disclosures before enabling the change. Contact: Ridha alkhater — rahaak9@gmail.com.

Support website hosting

This optional support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. This website hosting is separate from the offline game, which makes no connection to this site during gameplay. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Voluntary support messages

If you choose to email support, your email provider and the developer receive the information you choose to include so that your request can be answered. This is separate from gameplay; the game does not automatically send these messages or attach device identifiers. Do not include sensitive information unnecessary for support.
